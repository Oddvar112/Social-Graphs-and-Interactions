    What is being turned. Each page's TF-IDF vector (tf = count / words, idf = ln(303 / pages with the word), unit length) is the sum of three pieces: its names, its habit words and the rest. A dial multiplies one piece by its setting, and the vector is renormalised. The cosine of two pages is then a sum of the three parts shown in the bars, and they add up to the number on the right. At 100 and 100 this is the course's TF-IDF; at 0 and 100 it is TF-IDF with the names removed; at 0 and 0 names and habit words are both gone. linked means a Wikipedia link in either direction between the two pages in the week 1 snapshot.

    Names: words capitalised in the middle of sentences, or in a page title. Habit words: NLTK stopwords, and words on at least 101 of the 303 pages. The post audits both rules. Click a name in the list to move to that page. The state is in the address, so a link keeps your dials.
