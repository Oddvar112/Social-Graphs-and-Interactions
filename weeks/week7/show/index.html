    IN OTHER WORDS

    THE SEARCH ENGINE GAME SHOW

    The host reads a description of a Marvel character. You point at the right portrait. Two search engines play the same clue at the same time, against all 303 Wikipedia pages. Beat them.

    1. 1Ten clues an episode. Some are written in the words of the character's own Wikipedia page, some say the same facts in other words. You only find out which after you answer.
    2. 2One clue is about someone who isn't there. No page, no portrait. Spot it and hit NOBODY HERE. The machines can say it too, when no page stands out from the rest, and they are not always right about it.
    3. 3You pick from six; the machines search all 303 pages and lock in before you have read the clue. The six include whatever they picked. Each machine will tell you its top three and its answer once an episode: press ASK on its podium.
    4. 4One point a clue, for anyone who is right. After every clue, the instant replay shows the words and the passages each machine went on.

    Round 1 of 10

    Portraits are the lead images of the Wikipedia articles, loaded from Wikipedia; only their addresses are stored. The art belongs to Marvel and is shown for identification. Clues were written by us before any ranking was computed. KEYWORD is TF-IDF over the 303 pages; MEANING is mxbai-embed-large-v1 over 4,479 passages of at most 180 words. Each says nobody here when its number one does not stand out, by a rule fitted on these clues. How it works, and what we found.
