WEB-CRAWLER

303 Marvel characters from Wikipedia. 1784 directed links. You start at home, get sent somewhere out in the network, and have to find your way back. The map does not exist until you walk on it.

How it works. You can only follow links out of the page you are standing on, exactly like a reader clicking onward. Nodes you have never seen do not exist yet. Once you reach the mission target the objective flips: get home.
Par is the shortest possible route out and back, computed with BFS on the real graph. Matching par means you read the network as well as the network can be read. Spider-sense points at a step that lies on a shortest route (+3 steps). Web-zip undoes your last step (+2 steps).
Why is this hard? The graph is directed. A linking to B says nothing about B linking back to A.
