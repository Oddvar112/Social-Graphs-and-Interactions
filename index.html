WEB-CRAWLER

Pick Spider-Man, Wolverine or Doctor Strange. You get sent somewhere out in the network and have to click your way home along real Wikipedia links, one page at a time. The map only exists where you have walked, and your score is measured against the shortest route that actually exists in the graph.
